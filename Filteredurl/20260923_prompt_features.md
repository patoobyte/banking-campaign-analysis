You are evaluating a bank marketing campaign using a standardized coding framework.

Your task is to evaluate the communication style of the campaign consistently and objectively. The same criteria and scoring definitions must be applied regardless of which bank or campaign is being analyzed.

Do not judge the quality, effectiveness, attractiveness, or persuasiveness of the campaign. Do not evaluate the bank, its products, or its claims.

### SCORING RULES

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

Use the following general interpretation:

1 = very low / strongly toward the first pole
2 = low / somewhat toward the first pole
3 = balanced or intermediate
4 = high / somewhat toward the second pole
5 = very high / strongly toward the second pole

Do not give a score based on your personal preference. Base the score on observable characteristics of the language.

If there is insufficient evidence to score a dimension confidently, write "Insufficient evidence" rather than guessing.

### Important methodological rules

* Apply exactly the same definitions to every campaign.
* Do not compare this campaign with other campaigns.
* Do not reward or penalize a campaign because you personally prefer its style.
* Do not infer the bank's overall brand personality from a single campaign.
* Evaluate the actual language and communication presented in the supplied material.
* Distinguish between the campaign's subject matter and its communication style.
* Do not infer audience characteristics unless supported by the text.
* Do not infer the campaign creators' intentions.
* Do not evaluate campaign effectiveness.
* If a characteristic is ambiguous, explain the ambiguity rather than forcing a score.
